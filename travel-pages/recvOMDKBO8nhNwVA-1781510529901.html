Top 3 Recommended Attractions

Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is an iconic symbol of France and offers breathtaking views of Paris from its observation decks. Be sure to visit in the evening to see it beautifully illuminated.

2

Louvre Museum

Home to thousands of works of art, including the Mona Lisa, the Louvre is the world's largest art museum and a historic monument in Paris. Plan your visit early to avoid long lines and consider a guided tour for a richer experience.

3

Cathedrale Notre-Dame de Paris

This stunning Gothic cathedral is renowned for its architecture and history, making it a must-see in Paris. Although it is currently under restoration, you can still admire its exterior and the surrounding Île de la Cité.
