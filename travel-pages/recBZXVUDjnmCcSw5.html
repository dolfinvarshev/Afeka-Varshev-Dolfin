Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is an iconic symbol of Paris and offers breathtaking views of the city from its observation decks. Be sure to visit in the evening to see it beautifully illuminated.

Louvre Museum

Home to thousands of works of art, including the Mona Lisa, the Louvre Museum is a must-visit for art lovers and history enthusiasts alike. Plan your visit early in the day to avoid long lines.

Cathedrale Notre-Dame de Paris

This stunning Gothic cathedral is renowned for its architecture and historical significance. Although it is currently under restoration, the area around it is still worth exploring.
