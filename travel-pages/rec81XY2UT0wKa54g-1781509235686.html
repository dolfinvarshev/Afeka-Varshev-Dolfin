Top 3 Recommended Attractions

1

Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is an iconic symbol of France and offers breathtaking views of Paris from its observation decks. Be sure to visit in the evening when it sparkles with lights for a magical experience.

2

Louvre Museum

Home to thousands of works of art, including the Mona Lisa, the Louvre is the world's largest art museum and a historic monument in Paris. Plan your visit in advance to avoid long lines and consider a guided tour to enhance your experience.

3

Cathedrale Notre-Dame de Paris

This masterpiece of French Gothic architecture is renowned for its stunning facade and beautiful stained glass windows. Although it is currently under restoration, the area around it is still worth visiting for its historical significance and charm.
